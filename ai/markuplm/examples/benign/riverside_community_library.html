Welcome to Riverside Community Library

Our community library offers books, study tables, children's activities, and help finding local information. Visitors are welcome to browse the shelves and use the reading room without an appointment. This information page explains our opening hours, accessibility arrangements, and regular events. It does not collect passwords, banking details, or online membership payments.

The library is open Tuesday through Friday from nine in the morning until six in the evening, and Saturday from ten until four. It is closed on Sundays, Mondays, and public holidays. The entrance is beside the community garden, with a level approach from the pavement. A notice at the front desk lists any temporary changes to the timetable.

Borrowing and returning books

Residents can ask staff about a library card during staffed opening hours. Most books can be borrowed for three weeks, and renewals are available when another reader has not requested the title. Return borrowed items at the desk or through the covered returns slot beside the entrance. Staff can explain any special arrangements for large-print books, local-history material, and items borrowed from partner libraries.

Study spaces and accessibility

There are quiet tables near the reference shelves and a small group-work area beside the noticeboard. Please keep telephone calls outside the reading room. The building has step-free access, an accessible toilet, and seating with armrests. If you need a quieter visit or assistance reaching a shelf, speak to a member of staff. We welcome suggestions for making the space easier for everyone to use.

Events this month

The weekly reading group meets on Thursday afternoon to discuss a different short story. A family story session takes place on Saturday morning, followed by a simple craft activity. Events are free unless the printed programme clearly states otherwise. Places for small workshops can be reserved at the front desk. Children should be accompanied by a responsible adult throughout family sessions.

Getting help

For opening-hour enquiries, contact the library using the telephone number printed on your membership leaflet or visit the reception desk. Do not send personal account credentials in a message. Our staff can help locate a book, explain catalogue searches, and suggest reliable reference resources. The community noticeboard also carries information about local classes, volunteer opportunities, and public meetings in nearby venues.
