Introducing the Peppa Pig Afternoon Tea Bus Tour: a new London bus tour for kids

Brigit’s Bakery is pleased to introduce the official Peppa Pig Afternoon Tea Bus Tour - surely the best London bus tour for kids? For the very first time in the UK, Brigit’s Bakery is welcoming children on board their iconic afternoon tea bus tours for a fantastic day out for the kids and the grown-ups. Launching on 13 August 2020, this guided, kid-friendly London bus tour for kids will be the talk of the town!

Who is Peppa Pig?

Peppa Pig is a lovable but slightly bossy little pig. Peppa is four years old and lives with Mummy Pig, Daddy Pig, and her little brother George. She likes playing with her best friend Suzy Sheep, visiting Granny and Grandpa Pig and looking after George. Most of all, Peppa loves jumping up and down in muddy puddles, laughing and making loud snorting noises. Her adventures always end happily with loud snorts of laughter!

The show's short 5-minute episodes cover both every day topics that our children are more than already familiar with and also themes that parents would love their kids to learn more about. So from bird spotting to Chinese New Year, Peppa Pig has got you covered! And with more than 300 episodes, the show is guaranteed to always keep your children amused and entertained.

So what better idea than to give the kids a day they won't forget by bringing the iconic Peppa Pig to London and combining a child-friendly sightseeing tour with our stunning Brigit's Bakery Afternoon Tea? After all, when the little ones are happy and having a great time, it means mummy and daddy can also enjoy the view from the top-deck of our double-decker bus and indulge in our delicious afternoon tea at the same time!

Introducing our Peppa Pig Afternoon Tea London bus tour for kids

We are pleased to announce that we’re launching the very first Peppa Pig afternoon tea experience in London. Brigit’s Bakery and Entertainment One’s Peppa Pig are working in collaboration to create the Peppa Pig Afternoon Tea Bus Tour.

The Peppa Pig tour launches on 13 August and tours are available weekly from Thursday through to Sunday. Just like all our regular and themed Afternoon Tea Bus Tours, the tour lasts approximately 90 minutes (depending on traffic). But unlike our other bus tours, the Peppa Pig tours departs from and arrive at Somerset House .

Taking in the best views of Central London, the bus takes you past some of the most famous London landmarks. And that includes Big Ben, The Houses of Parliament, Westminster Abbey, Hyde Park, Nelson’s Column, Tower Bridge, St Paul's Cathedral, Borough Market, Green Park, Piccadilly Circus and more.

If you've joined us on one of our tours before, you may already know that we don't normally have loos on board of our Brigit's Bakery Routemaster vintage buses. However, with the Peppa Pig Afternoon Tea Bus Tour, we pulled out all the stops! A child-friendly emergency loo available onboard makes our Peppa Pig bus tour perfect for children aged 2+. Please note it will be for use by children only.

Stunning decorations on our Peppa Pig Afternoon Tea bus

Our Peppa Pig themed double-decker bus is gracing the streets of Central London from 13th August 2020. And you won't be able to miss it!

The classic red London bus is a fully Peppa Pig immersive experience. The exterior features colourful character decorations and the interior has fun decals and brightly coloured furnishings, so little ones can feel as though they’re stepping into Peppa’s world. We are sure that it will become one of the most popular London bus tours for kids our fabulous city has to offer!

And the cherry on top? Special tablets are available on each table to provide a Peppa Pig guided animation of the London tour. So prepare for the children to be excited with sing-a-long songs, drawing activities and more in their special Peppa Pig Afternoon Tea Bus Tour Activity Book. And in case you’re wondering, the animations are based on this popular London-themed episode, which you can watch below.

Our bus is also available as a Peppa Pig party bus hire , with bespoke packages to suit your requirements. So keep an eye out on our website for more information on that to be released soon.

Which safety measures have been put in place for social distancing?

In accordance with the Public Health England (PHE) guidelines, we are excited to welcome you all back on board the Brigit’s Bakery Afternoon Tea Bus Tours very soon. The schedule of bus tours will be limited in the shorter term as the safety and peace of mind of our guests and staff remain our utmost priority. We have put in place some additional precautionary measures in line with the Health & Safety Executive (HSE):

  • The bus tours will run at reduced capacity to comply with social distancing guidelines. Each party will be seated with the required distance from other guests.
  • Staff will be wearing masks at all times. Guests will not be required to wear masks on board as we serve food and drink during the afternoon tea bus tours.
  • Passengers are required to wear masks when boarding, but are permitted to remove them during the tour.
  • Staff will be wearing disposable gloves.
  • Hand sanitiser will be provided on board and all guests will be asked to sanitise their hands on entering and exiting the bus.
  • As always, guests will be served their beverages in their own brand new reusable cup which they are encouraged to take home after the tour.
  • Refills will be done one at a time to further reduce any potential risk for cross contamination across parties.
  • The lead booker must have full contact details of each person in their booking for any potential contact tracing requirements.
  • Guests will be boarded and seated on to the bus from the front to back, and will be asked to exit in reverse order from back to front in order to further minimise close contact between guests.

We will continue to take guidance from the HSE and PHE and implement any changes required.

We thank you for your understanding and ongoing support and look forward to seeing you soon.
