Reset Forgotten Password - Account Management

Reset Forgotten Password - Account Management

Introduction

 

This article provides step-by-step instructions on how to recover a forgotten GCC account password.

Guidelines

You can watch the video below for a tutorial. 


Or, you can check the step by step guidelines below. 
 

  1. Go to  www.globalsign.com  and click  Log In .
  2. Click on the  Forgot your Password?   Click Here  link to start the password reset process.
  3. Enter your User Name and create a temporary password. Click  Next . Your User ID is the PARXXXX_xxxxx number given to you at the end of the account setup as well as in the Welcome Email. If you have forgotten your User ID as well you will need to contact GlobalSign support.
  4. As the screen indicates you will be sent an email to your registered email address. Please access the link in the email to complete the password reset process. Click  Close .
  5. Click the reset link or copy and paste the link into a browser window.
  6. Enter your temporary password and click  Next .
  7. Enter and re-enter a new password for your account. Click  Next .
  8. You have successfully reset your password and will be logged into your GCC account.
