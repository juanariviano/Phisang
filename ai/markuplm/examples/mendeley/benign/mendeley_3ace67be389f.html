4

Close-up works for me and it also works for me that the price is lower than some other brands. I specifically like the Cinnamon flavor close up. I really don't like the taste of mint and using any mint toothpaste is unpleasant for me. Close Up has a flavor labeled Ultra Cinnamon flavor blast.
