Make a Yahoo Japan email account

Hi! So for the Yahoo ticket lottery often you will need a Yahoo Japan email account. I will go through the process.

Requirements: Ability to read Japanese Hirgana

Step 1. Go to Yahoo Japan.
https://account.edit.yahoo.co.jp/registration?.src=www&.done=https%3A%2f%2fwww.yahoo.co.jp

This URL will bring you to the main registration page.

  • 連絡用メールアドレス: Contact email address, input a secondary email address to use
  • Yahoo JAPAN ID: Pick an ID for your account. This will show on your yahoo email address as well as be how you log in. This cannot be changed so choose wisely!
  • パスワード: Password.
  • 郵便番号: Postal code. You can use the one in this screenshot or you can look up any random Japanese address. I recommend looking up the address of Tokyo Dome or any landmark and using that post code.


  • 生年月日: Birthday. Input this in the YYYY/MM/DD, the / will automatically appear when you type it in.
  • 性別: Sex, 男性 is man and 女性 is woman
  • 配信を希望する: If you want to receive information emails/ads keep this box checked. If not, uncheck it.
  • T カード: It’s just a point card. select 持っていない (do not have) for this.
  • 画像の文字: Enter in the hiragana characters you see on screen. If you do not have Japanese input you can use this  tool to help you. If you do not know how to read Japanese hiragana you can find a chart here to help you.

Click the yellow button at the bottom of the screen to continue.


Here you will be asked to just confirm the information you inputted. Once that is done, click the yellow button at the bottom that says “同意して登録する” (accept the terms and conditions and register).

Step 2. Verify your contact email.

Once you successfully register you will be asked to confirm your account. The easiest way is just to check the secondary email you signed up with.

You should get an email that looks like this. Once you get it, just click the link and your account will be confirmed and ready to use! Enjoy!
