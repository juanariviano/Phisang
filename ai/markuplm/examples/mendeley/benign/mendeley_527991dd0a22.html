Westlife fans are raging after losing out on tickets due to 'network error' this morning

‘My nerves are shot.’

By Niamh McClelland Thursday 25 Oct 2018, 10:14 AM
Oct 25th 2018, 10:14 AM 7,007 Views 2 Comments
http://dailyedge.ie/4304697
Share 121 Tweet Email

AFTER EIGHT LONG years, Westlife are returning to the stage next summer – as a four-piece they hastened to add at a press conference earlier this week.

Source: PA Wire/PA Images

Tickets went on sale for dates across the UK and Ireland this morning, and anyone who loves a good key change was ready and waiting to part with their hard-earned cash.

Unfortunately, technical difficulties seemed to have gotten between fans and the foursome, meaning many were left devastated as error messages thwarted their best intentions.

If you’re among those bitterly disappointed this morning, you might find solace in your friends – both here and across the pond – below.
