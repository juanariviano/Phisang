    Services

    What we can do for you

    Fully mobile responsive Website

    Mobile Internet usage has exploded in recent years, with over 50% of people browsing Websites on portable devices. With this in mind, the specialist team at Whistlefish integrate fluid, responsive design into every Website we produce

    CMS sites

     

    Our Content Management System allows you to take the steering wheel of your Website. Giving you control over the content and many other aspects of your website.

    Company branding & logo design

    Our creative team offer logo design and branding as a standalone service or they can be bundled into the creation of your new Website.

    eCommerce Website


    More people are shopping online than ever. We offer fully customisable eCommerce solutions, designed to make your business succeed online.

    Social Media

    Social media can be a very powerful tool for your online marketing strategies. At Whistlefish, we can set up and manage multiple social media channels for your company to increase brand recognition.

    SEO

    We  provide basic SEO (search engine optimisation) as part of your Website package, and can provide 'on page' optimisation to vastly improve your Website ranking in search engines and drive more potential customers to your business.

    Sage Integration

    We offer full integration of our eCommerce system into Sage 200 and Sage 50 Accounts systems. We can also integrate into many other back office systems.

    Bespoke Web Solutions

    First impressions count for everything, our Websites are bespoke designs tailored to your business providing you with an aesthetically pleasing Website designed to increase your ROI.
