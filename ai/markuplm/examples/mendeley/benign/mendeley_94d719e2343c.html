 Automated Surface Observing System (ASOS)


Description:Hourly weather conditions such as air temperature, humidity, winds, precipitation, visibility, and pressure are recorded by these automated sensors. ASOS arrays, maintained by National Weather Service, are often located at airports.
Status:Active - 1996 to Present
Number of Sites in Program:313 in eastern US
Number of Sites Registered in GCMP:223
Point of Contact:No Point of Contact Recorded
Program URL: http://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/weather/asos/
Data Access URL: http://www.nc-climate.ncsu.edu/cronos
Sponsoring Organization(s):National Weather Service, Federal Aviation Administration
Management Issue Addressed/Purpose of Sampling:Air traffic operations
Sampling Frequency:Hourly
Area Sampled:National
Monitored Parameters:MediaMeasurementBiologicalChemicalPhysical
