Security and Browser Requirements

Security Issues:

To help maintain security, you should close your browser when you have completed your Internet banking session. This will help prevent unauthorized persons from viewing your private account information.

Never share your password.

Browser Requirements

To help ensure security and proper functionality, you must use a browser that meets or exceeds the following requirements:

  • 128 bit encryption
  • You must have Cookies and JavaScript enabled
  • We support the latest browser versions of Internet Explorer, Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Opera.

We recommend you update your browser to the latest version. With our new website update, we’d like to remind you to update your browser. Updating your browser ensures you have the most up-to-date security and performance. New browsers have many advantages:

  • Newer browsers have updated security against the most recent security risks. With an up-to-date browser, malicious websites have a tougher time accessing and damaging your computer through your browser
  • Bugs are constantly being fixed and updated to provide a cleaner, sleeker, faster user experience
  • Newer technologies allow you to use more features easier
  • Older browsers may not be supported by some websites

Help for Homeowners

If you are having difficulty making payments on your Poppy Bank mortgage loan or home equity line of credit and are looking to avoid foreclosure, please contact us today. We would be happy to talk with you and discuss possible options.

If you are interested in discussing possible options, please call us toll-free at 1-888-636-9994. Be prepared to discuss your household income and expenses, and have the following documentation available when you call:

  • Recent pay stubs
  • A list of your current outstanding debts and minimum monthly payments
  • Your most recent tax return
  • Most recent bank statements and/or brokerage statements
  • A current valuation of your property such as an appraisal, if available
  • Every situation is different, and we cannot guarantee that our efforts will be successful in avoiding foreclosure.

You may also contact the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) to find a HUD-certified housing counseling agency by calling 1-800-569-4287. HUD is a U.S. government agency that increases home ownership and supports home buyers. HUD offers low-cost counseling throughout the country. The following additional resources provide information and links to agencies and organizations that may also be able to help you:

  • HUD - US Dept. Of Housing And Urban Development
  • FDIC - Federal Deposit Insurance Corp.
  • FRB - Federal Reserve Board
