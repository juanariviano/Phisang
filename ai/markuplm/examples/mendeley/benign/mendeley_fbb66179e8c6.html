Appointment Request - Only for D Pharm/B Pharm /Pharm D from other than Maharashtra State
Applicants passing from Out of Maharashtra State or Who are Registered Pharmacist of other state and wants to seek transfer to Maharashtra state are requested to click here for sending Appointment Request - Only for D Pharm/B Pharm /Pharm D from other than Maharashtra State to seek appointment schedule for document verification of their documents in person at MSPC office. After in person document verification at MSPC office they are requested to refer to www.mspcindia.org and then submit application by online mode. All concerned are requested to note changes w.e.f 21/02/2019 mentioned on www.mspcindia.org
